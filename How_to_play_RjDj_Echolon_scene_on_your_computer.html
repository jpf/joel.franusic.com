How to play Echolon (from RjDj) on your computer

While it isn't quite as neat as running it on an iPhone, here is how to listen to the RjDj Echolon Scene on your computer:

  1. Install Pure Data
  2. Open Echolon
    1. Do It Yourself
      1. Download Echolon from the RjDj SVN repository

        $ svn co http://rjdj.me/svn/rjdj_scenes/trunk/rjdj_scenes/Echolon.rj/
